1. Product Overview & Executive Summary 
SevaNest is a proposed simple, trusted and scalable household workforce platform connecting customers with verified 
maids, drivers, cooks, babysitters, elderly-care helpers and house-cleaning workers. The platform supports recurring 
household employment as well as short-term service requirements. 
The product consists of a Customer Mobile App, Worker Mobile App and Web-based Admin Panel. The first production 
release is planned within four months (16 weeks), with parallel work across product design, mobile applications, 
backend, admin portal, integrations, testing and deployment. 
The core product principle is simplicity: customers should be able to find a suitable worker in a few steps, while workers 
should be able to register, verify identity, specify availability and receive job opportunities without navigating a complex 
interface.

1.1 Product Name Rationale 
"Seva" represents service/help, while "Nest" represents the home. The name is broad enough to include maids, drivers, 
cooks, caregivers and future home-service categories without restricting the brand to one role. 
Working product name: SevaNest. Trademark, company-name and domain availability must be verified before public 
launch. 
1.2 Product Goals 
 Make household staff discovery and hiring simple and structured. 
 Create a trusted worker-verification and customer-verification process. 
 Support recurring monthly employment as well as daily/hourly requirements. 
 Provide interview, booking, payment, attendance, rating and replacement workflows. 
 Give operations teams complete control through an admin dashboard. 
 Build a scalable technology base for multi-city expansion.

9. Trust, Verification & Safety Framework 
Trust is the highest-risk area in this business model. The platform should never present a worker as fully verified until 
the configured checks have been completed and approved by an authorized operations user. 
Verification Version 1 Handling 
Mobile OTP verification 
Identity Government ID upload and manual/partner verification 
Address Document/reference verification 
Emergency Contact Captured and verified where operationally feasible 
Previous Employment Reference details and status 
Police Verification Status/document tracking; external process as applicable 
Customer Verification Mobile, address and basic profile checks 
Audit Trail Admin action, status and timestamps retained

23. Product Recommendation / North Star 
SevaNest should be treated as a four-month flagship VPD project with a disciplined Version 1 scope. The 
recommended contractual duration is 16 weeks, covering requirements, design, development, integration, testing, UAT, 
deployment and initial stabilization. 
The product should be built around trust and operational simplicity rather than excessive features. The strongest launch 
proposition is: verified household staff, simple booking, transparent status and dependable replacement support.
